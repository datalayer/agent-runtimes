Working hours. The desk is open from nine to five, Monday to Friday. Nobody is expected to answer outside those hours, and a message sent on Friday evening is read on Monday.

Days off. Everybody has twenty-five days off a year, taken in whole or half days. Ask your lead a week ahead for one day, a month ahead for a week or more.

Expenses. Keep the receipt and file it within the month. Anything over two hundred euros is agreed before it is spent; a train is always agreed, a taxi needs a reason.

Laptops. A laptop is replaced every four years, or sooner when it fails. Report a lost one the same day: the disk is encrypted, and the account is locked from the admin console.

Meetings. A meeting has an owner, an agenda sent a day ahead, and notes sent the same day. Thirty minutes unless the agenda says why it needs more.

Code review. Every change is read by somebody else before it is merged. The reader answers within a working day, and says what they would change rather than what they would have written.
