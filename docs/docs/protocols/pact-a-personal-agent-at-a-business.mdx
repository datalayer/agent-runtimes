# A Personal Agent at a Business

<ProtocolDeck id="pact-a-personal-agent-at-a-business" />

A case of [PACT](./pact), told in a reader's words and played with a demo
airline. It is the Protocols tab's sixth case on the
[Datalayer home page](https://datalayer.ai); the Personal Agent Protocol's four
are [in its documentation](https://personal-agent-protocol.datalayer.tech/examples/cases/).
[PAP and PACT side by side](./pap-and-pact) compares the two protocols.

The deck is exported at `@datalayer/agent-runtimes/lib/protocols/decks`, as YAML
and parsed, in the format of Datalayer Decks.
